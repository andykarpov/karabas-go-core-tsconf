# CLAUDE.md

This file provides guidance to Claude Code (claude.ai/code) when working with code in this repository.

## Overview

The TS-Conf ZX Spectrum core for the Karabas Go, Mini and Mini G boards: Xilinx Spartan-6 XC6SLX25, ISE 14.7 (XST). One core, three tops: `src/rtl/karabas_go_top.v` (VGA through the FT812 / video DAC, floppy connector), `karabas_mini_top.v` and `karabas_minig_top.v` (HDMI, the FT812 video on HDMI too). The machine is `src/rtl/tsconf.v` (TS-Conf of ZX Evolution with General Sound, TurboSound (two AY / YM, the FM part taken out), OPL4 (ZXM-MoonSound: FM + wavetable), Covox, ZiFi / RS232 over USB, the FDC of the Go; the SAA1099, the FM of the TS-FM (jt12) and the OPL2 (jtopl) were taken out for the OPL4; the sources of the SAA1099, jt12 and jtopl are still in `rtl/sound`, the OPL2 glue (`opl2_top.v`, `opl2_clk.v`) is deleted); the tops differ in their board glue and the defines HW_ID1 (Go), HW_ID2 (Mini), HW_ID2 + HW_ID3 (Mini G), set in the `.xst` files.

The board modules (MCU link, memories, clocks, DACs, OSD, HDMI) come from the git submodule `modules/` ([karabas-go-modules](https://github.com/andykarpov/karabas-go-modules), shared by the Karabas Go cores; clone with `--recursive` or run `git submodule update --init`). A change to them is made there and taken here by updating the submodule commit. Its `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/PLAN.md` have the rules (Verilog-2001 for XST, tests, checks of LUTs and timing). `src/rtl/fd1793` is another submodule, not used by the build (the FDC is `src/rtl/vg93`).

## Build

- `cd src && make` builds the three boards (`make go`, `mini`, `minig` one of them; `make minig-xst`, `-impl`, `-core` one step; `make minig-report` the utilization and timing of the last build, `make minig-coremode` the second timing run of the Mini / Mini G, see Timing). It runs the ISE command-line flow (the steps and options of the former ISE projects) and sources `/opt/Xilinx/14.7/ISE_DS/settings64.sh` (`ISE_SETTINGS=` to change it); on Windows (msys make from Git Bash) the nt64 tools of `C:/Xilinx/14.7/ISE_DS` (`XILINX_DIR=`), the 32-bit bitgen. Near-full boards: `src/tools/par_multi.sh` (from core-quake: cost tables in parallel, at most 2, the first that meets wins, copies on `D:/.claude/karabas-go-core-tsconf/par`; `BOARD=minig tools/par_multi.sh 4 5` after `make minig-xst`). The boards share `xst/` and `_ngo/` in `src/`, so they are built one after the other; a board takes 10 to 30 minutes, mostly PAR.
- Inputs per board: `karabas_<board>.prj` (the source list, add new files here; the shared modules as `"../modules/rtl/..."`), `.xst`, `.ucf`, `.ut`. There is no ISE GUI project (`.xise`) any more; the Makefile has its map / par options. The map cost table (the placement seed) is per board, `MAP_T_<board>` (2 on the Go, 5 on the Mini G since 26112601 (4 before, since 26112001: with 2 an OPL4 path of clk_sys missed by 0.39 ns); 5 on the Mini since 26112201: 2 missed by 2.55 ns, 4 by 0.24 ns, 3 hung in the router): when a build of the nearly full Mini / Mini G misses a constraint by a placement (a path without logic and a long route), try another (2...5) before changing the design.
- `core` packs `karabas_<board>.bin` and the ROMs of `core.json` (one file for all boards: the common keys, `boards` for what differs; kgo2, karabas-go-tools `docs/kgo2.md`) into `tsconf.kg` (`make <board>-core`: `make_core.py core.json tsconf.kg --boards <board>` puts that board's bitstream in) with `make_core.py`, a symlink to karabas-go-tools (the sibling repo must be checked out next to this one). The `.bin` and `.kg` files are committed as the release.
- The `osd` items of the json are the soft switch registers in their order (`kg_soft_switches` in the tops cut the fields out). A new item changes the index of the items after it.
- The Mini and Mini G are nearly full (build 26112201: Mini 13830 LUT = 92 %, Mini G 13485 = 89 % (26112601: 13468), 99 % of the slices; Go 12226 = 81 %, 98 % slices; the island encoder of zhdmi in flip-flops, modules #26, took about 350 LUT off). Check `karabas_<board>_map.mrp` and `karabas_<board>.par` after a change (and `karabas_<board>_core.twr` of the Mini / Mini G); `make <board>-report` prints them.

## Timing

- Clocks (the tops), one PLL (VCO 925 MHz), phase 0: clk_sys 84.09 MHz (SRAM controller, the SDRAM of the sound, the wavetable part of the OPL4), clk_bus 28.03 MHz (the machine; clk_sys / 3, every edge of clk_bus is an edge of clk_sys; its PLL output has no BUFG in kg_pll, the BUFG is in the top and on the Mini / Mini G the same output feeds the BUFGMUX of `kg_hdmi_ft_pll`), clk_8 7.97 MHz (FT_CLK_OUT and DCM_CLKGEN of the Mini / Mini G; no loads on the Go), 12 MHz (MIDI). `rtl/bus_ce.v` makes ce_28m (the clk_sys enable before each clk_bus edge, for the SRAM controller) and v_clk (V_CLK of the Go).
- HDMI: `kg_hdmi_ft` of modules (`rtl/video/hdmi`, made from the former `rtl/hdmi/hdmi_top.v` of this core; CLKRATE 28030303, REF_MHZ 28): the FT812 / core mux, the audio sample transfer, the crossings into the pixel clock, around zhdmi (`hdmi_tx`). `kg_hdmi_ft_pll` (with `kg_freq_counter`) measures FT_CLK and programs DCM_CLKGEN (8 MHz x M / D) for the FT modes; the pixel clock is one BUFGMUX of clk_bus (its PLL output) and the DCM. The 5x clock of the -2 part allows FT modes up to 64 MHz; 72 and 80 MHz are beyond the CLKFX limit.
- The crossings into the pixel clock: the FT812 video is taken at the falling FT_CLK (in the IOB) and goes through `kg_async_fifo` (same frequency, unknown phase), whose write side runs on the falling FT_CLK too (half a period of 80 MHz is shorter than the route from the IOB). What goes from clk_bus (the core video, ft_sel, audio_en, the reset of hdmi_tx, the audio sample) leaves registers on the falling clk_bus: in the mode of the core the BUFGMUX output is about 4 ns later than the BUFG of clk_bus. In the FT mode ft_sel / audio_en / the reset go through two flip-flops, the audio sample with a toggle.
- The ucf bounds clk_bus -> pixel clock as an asynchronous crossing (TS_BUS_TO_PIX), as it is in the FT mode; `make <board>-coremode` runs trce again with the FT-mode constraints taken out of the pcf (`src/pcf_core.py`), which checks the same paths with the setup and hold of clk_bus. Run it after every build of the Mini / Mini G. The BUFGMUX select has a TIG (a KEEP net `mux_sel`; INST TNM on a BUFGMUX is not supported).
- ESP_CS of the Mini G (`ESP32_SPI_CS_N`, K5, only the PULLUP of the pad): #77 with D0 = 0, D7 = 1 hands the FT812 SPI to the ESP32 and lets the pin go to Z; the ESP signals the end by a low pulse (~2 us), read as #77 bit 7 (`espcs_int` of `zports.v`). The line rises slowly from the 0 of #13, so the latch takes espcs_in through two flip-flops and is armed only after 15 clocks high since the handover (a level latch gave a false completion right after #82; DOOM worked around it with a second #82). `tb_eq_zports` checks bit 7 against a model.
- MCU_SCK of the Mini G goes through IBUFG + BUFG (on local routing PAR could not meet hold); the SCK of the FT812 SPI, not a clock load, takes the IBUFG output.
- The three ucf have the same timing block (the end of each file, then the parts of the board): PERIOD on CLK_50MHZ (derived onto the PLL outputs), TS_CLK_MCU, DATAPATHONLY bounds for the asynchronous crossings (MCU_SCK <-> clk_bus, clk_bus <-> clk_8 on the Mini / Mini G, FT_CLK, ESP32 I2S), the SRAM budget (12 ns out + 10 ns tAA + 12 ns in in three clk_sys), the request of the arbiter into `sram.v` (TS_BUS_TO_SRAM_CYC: taken only with cyc = ce & c3, three clk_sys after the clk_bus edge), the FT pixel clock with its own PERIOD (64 MHz), TS_BUS_TO_PIX and the bounds of the fifo (TS_FT_TO_PIX, TS_PIX_TO_FT) and of FT_CLK <-> clk_bus. Each exception is explained there; do not TIG paths that are real.
- No clocks from logic and no latches (check "Clock Information" of the `.syr`): the registers on the Z80 clock in `zports.v` run on clk_bus with zpos, the IDE trigger latches are registers with a bypass, the FDC (`hrdy_fdc`, the VG93 model, `fdd_glue`) runs on clk_bus. A register whose initial value differs from its asynchronous reset value makes XST build a latch (`video_ports.v`).

## Simulation

- `src/sim/run_eq_sim.sh` (iverilog, ISim): the rewrites of the timing work against the old code in `sim/ref` (the versions of cec5631, modules renamed `*_ref`): `tb_eq_zports` (#77 bit 7 against a model of the ESP_CS latch instead), `tb_eq_saa`, and `tb_bus_ce` (with the PLL model). Each prints `<tb>: 0 errors`.
- `src/sim/run_fdc_sim.sh` (iverilog): `tb_hrdy_fdc`, the FDC of the Go with a model of a drive (restore, read and write a sector, the TR-DOS poll loop), the CPU in turbo and at 3.5 MHz; the same test as in the Profi core. Then `tb_fdc_z80s` (about a minute): the same drive (`fdc_drive.vh`) with z80-silicon running `fdc_z80s.asm` (Restore, Read / Write Sector by INI / OUTI, the poll of #FF) at 14 / 7 / 3.5 MHz; `-DCPU_T80PA` runs it with T80pa (every number the same).
- `src/sim/run_hdmi_sim.sh` (ISim): `tb_hdmi_top`, `kg_hdmi_ft` of modules as in the tops: the core video at 28 MHz and the FT812 at 40 / 72 MHz through `kg_hdmi_ft_pll` and the fifo, every pixel where it enters `hdmi_tx`, the audio samples and their rate. zhdmi runs as a ghdl `--synth` netlist (ISim 14.7 stops in `zhdmi_island_encoder.vhd`).
- `src/sim/run_burst_sim.sh` (ISim): `tb_mcu_burst.vhd`, the CMD_CAPS answer and the rom loader over classic frames and bursts at 16 / 32 MHz, `kg_mcu` with the parameters of the tops.
- `src/sim/run_sdram_sim.sh` (iverilog, ISim): `tb_sdram2p`, `sdram2p` with both ports busy against a reference (and the DQ of the controller and the SDRAM never at once, the refresh interval), and `tb_gs_top`, the GS with its cache running `gs_memtest.asm` (sjasmplus) while the OPL4 and the FL loader use port 2; it prints the GS speed in a loop of cache hits (14.00 MHz). Both with `sdram_model.v` (from karabas-go-core-archie, CL 3, timing checks).
- `src/sim/run_opl4_sim.sh` (iverilog, about a minute): `tb_opl4`, `opl4_kg` with a Z80 (tv80) running `opl4/opl4_z80.asm` (the chip ID, the RAM detect of the ZXM-MoonSound service program, a sample upload with its verify, a read of the ROM loaded by the FL loader, the FM timer IRQ, the FM registers read back) through port 2 of `gs_top`; the FM part a stub. It also counts the engine frame (clocks of the 1907 of a sample) and the voice samples played without the interpolation (`DEADLINE`). Then `tb_opl4_lpf` with `opl4_lpf.py`: the components other than a 5 / 15 kHz tone at the PCM5102 rate in the three modes of `audio_lpf`, at the rates of the OPL4 and of the GS.
- `src/sim/opl4ref/run.sh <yrw801.rom>` (Verilator, about 4 minutes): `opl4.v` against ymfm ymf278b (cloned into `sim/build/ymfm_src`), scripts of `gen.py` per part (plain env lfo rev tl multi rand), with the interpolation off (ymfm as it is) and on (ymfm patched with the formula of openMSX, `pcm_interp.py`); every sample bit exact. Run with `-GDEADLINE=999999` (no fallback).
- `src/sim/opl3ref/run.sh <disk.trd>...` (Verilator, about 3 minutes): the FM part `opl3_fm` against ymfm ymf262 on the OPL instruments of MoonBlaster songs (`patches.py`; sample-exact compare is not possible, the LFO phases differ): `noise.py` the noise outside the harmonics of each note (ymfm, RTL, the resampling to 44.1 kHz modelled), then `tb_kg.cpp` (`opl4_kg` itself, FM -> OUT_L) with `kgnoise.py`, with and without the FM interpolation.
- `src/sim/hw` (hardware tests, TRDs for TR-DOS, run from WC: mount on A, RESET with BOOT): `opl4t.asm` (`gen_opl4t.py`: the sample RAM written / read back, the sine in the three formats), `opl4r.asm` (`gen_opl4r.py <rom>`: the YRW801 read back from the SDRAM, a Fletcher sum per 64 KB, green / red), `opl4z.asm` (the sample RAM zeroed), `bordt.asm` (the border on the even ports, A0 = 0: 9 bands, the OPL4 ports must not take it, the beeper only on #FE).
- `src/sim/run_z80s_sim.sh` (Verilator, about a minute): z80-silicon (`rtl/z80-silicon`, github.com/dr-Titus/z80-silicon-rtl 1.0.0, GPL v3; `gen_cen.py` makes `z80_silicon_cen.v`, one clock with the enables cen_p / cen_n instead of its two clock edges, modules `z80s_*`): `tb_z80s_cen` (the converted core against the original, every output at every clock) and `tb_z80s_t80` (against T80pa of `tsconf.v` as a ghdl netlist: the bus at every clock and the registers; the header lists the differences found, all T80pa against the chip). Verilator, as the core has registers without an initial value (X in iverilog).
- `src/sim/run_esp_audio_sim.sh` (iverilog, about a minute): `tb_esp_audio`, the ESP32 audio of the Mini G: an I2S source at 44.1 kHz + 50 ppm -> `i2s_rx` -> `audio_interp` -> `audio_lpf` in the three modes, taken at 54.7 / 48 kHz; every frame received, the images below 20 kHz (`opl4_lpf.py`, an FFT of every bin).
- `src/sim/run_vga_sim.sh` (iverilog, seconds): `tb_vga_start_hold`, the start hold of the Go VGA.
- `src/sim/run_kbd_sim.sh` (ISim): `tb_kbd_rtc.vhd`, `kg_mcu` and `hid_parser` as in the tops: PS/2 scancodes from the MCU read by the CPU through the RTC registers F0..FF (the keyboard buffer of `usb_ps2_keybuf.v`, on after register 0C bit 7 = 0; Wild Commander and others read the keyboard so), the buffer reset, and a USB report on the key matrix.
- The tests of the shared modules are in `modules/sim` (`run_all.sh`).

## Layout

- `src/rtl/tsconf.v` and `src/rtl/tsconf/`: the TS-Conf machine (zclock, zports, zmem, dram, video, dma, ...); `tune.v` its defines.
- `src/rtl/karabas_*_top.v`: the boards: PLL, `bus_ce`, `kg_mcu`, `kg_soft_switches`, `kg_cursor`, `kg_osd_overlay`, the DACs, HDMI (`kg_hdmi_ft`). The NMI of the Z80 (`nmi_n` of `tsconf.v`): the OSD item NMI (F11) or BTN2, registered on clk_bus; TS-Conf has no NMI ROM page, the CPU goes to 0066 of the memory paged in (the 48K ROM resets on it).
- The VGA of the Go: the syncs of `video_sync.v` are high active (`kg_osd_overlay` needs that); the top inverts them on the pins by the OSD item "VGA Sync" (index 5, Go only; default -H -V as 576p, a TV took +H for -H and moved the picture by the width of the pulse). The H pulse is 64 px (`HSYNCV_END`), the picture starts 148 px after its start. The items after it have an explicit `index` in core.json (the dead "VGA Hsync 50/60 Hz" was removed, ENABLE_60HZ is off), so a board left out keeps its numbers. `rtl/vga_start_hold.v` (Go, before the overlay): black and no syncs while the PLL is unlocked or `BUSY` of `kg_mcu2` (the INIT of the core), then 32 frames more, then the picture from the start of a vsync pulse (a TV shifted the picture after every core start; not confirmed on that TV yet).
- `src/rtl/mcu`: the core-specific parts of the HID input (`hid_parser.vhd`, `usb_ps2_keybuf.v`), `dot_matrix.v` (Mini G).
- The CPUs: z80-silicon (`rtl/z80-silicon`, see Simulation) for the machine (`z80s_t80pa` with the ports of T80pa; `Z80_SILICON` of `tune.v`, off = T80pa) and for the GS (`gs.v` parameter `SILICON`, 0 = T80s: the page `mem` from the address at once and a memory write = MREQ without RD / RFSH, because the chip samples WAIT at the fall of T2, see `gs_top.v`). NMOS: OUT (C),0.
- `src/rtl/sound`: GS (`gs.v`, `gs_top.v`), TurboSound (`turbosound.v`, two `kg_ym2149` of modules: the AY-3-8910 `ay38910_silicon.v` after the schematic of dr.Titus, checked against the transistor-level model of lvd2 there; R13 restarts the envelope period, the measured AY DAC levels, quieter in the middle than the `ym2149.v` of MikeJ / Sorgelig before; the MIDI out on port A of the first), the OPL4 (`opl4/`), Covox, the mixer.
- The SDRAM of the sound (`rtl/ram/sdram2p.v`, from the MiST controller, clk_sys): a cycle of 8 clocks, CL 3, port 1 (banks 0 / 1) the GS, port 2 (banks 2 / 3) the OPL4 sample memory (4 MB: the YRW801 ROM, the sample RAM); the read data on the rising edge of clk_sys (SDR_CLK is clk_sys inverted; the falling edge missed setup by ~2 ns at the slow Go corner, BaseConf #20), an init of ~390 us with 8 AUTO REFRESH (the SDRAM is not powered off between cores); its `ready` -> `gs_top` `sdram_ready` -> `LD_READY` of `kg_mcu2` (CAPS bit 4: the ROM loader of the GS has one byte of buffer, the FL bytes a queue of 4). A port 2 write waits for a cycle without a port 1 read (DQ). The GS Z80 has a read cache of 512 words in `gs_top.v` and WAIT (`MEM_WAIT` of `gs.v`) on a miss (about 3 wait states), the writes are posted.
- The OPL4 (`rtl/sound/opl4`, from u16_tsconf_gs_v24): `opl4.v` the wavetable part (bit exact to ymfm, clk_sys), `opl3/` the FM part (opl3_fpga, LGPL-3, SystemVerilog: `opl3/gen_v.sh` makes `opl3_fm_gen.v` for XST with sv2v; run it after a change of the `.sv` and commit both), `opl4_kg.v` the ports #C4..#C7, #7E / #7F (not in DOS). The FM registers read back through #C5 / #C7 (a copy in `opl4.v`, as openMSX; ymfm returns FF): the MFM player (MoonBlaster FM + Wave) keys FM notes off by IN / AND / OUT of B0, with FF every release went to block 7 (a hiss, 26111604). The OSD item "OPL4 Interp" (index 16, default on) switches the linear interpolation of the samples (the formula of openMSX; a per-channel cache of two samples, a voice falls back to none when the frame would end after `DEADLINE`) and of the FM output (the FM engine at 49.5 kHz on even ticks, interpolated at the 44.1 kHz tick between its last two samples; before, the last finished sample was taken and one in 8 dropped: noise under bright FM tones). The YRW801 ROM (2 MB) comes from the SD card: the FL item "OPL4 ROM" of core.json (slot 1, `/tsconf/yrw801.rom`, autoload), the tops pass `ld_wr` of that slot as `fl_*`; the OPL4 is held while it loads. Its output (44.1 kHz), the GS (a sample per INT of the GS, 37.47 kHz; `SAMPLE_T` of `gs.v`) and on the Mini G the ESP32 (I2S 44.1 kHz of its clock, `sample_t` of `i2s_rx`) go through `rtl/sound/audio_lpf.v` (clk_bus; the sum 18 bits on the Mini G, 17 on the Go / Mini): the OSD item "GS/OPL4 Filter" (index 14) off / linear interpolation / interpolation + two 1-pole low-pass stages; the DACs and HDMI sample the mix at 54.7 / 48 kHz without a filter, so the steps of 44.1 kHz fold down into the band (a "sand"). The mixer saturates its 18-bit sum (it wrapped around before) and is registered. The OSD item "Tape monitor" (index 15: Off / In / Out / Both) mixes the tape input and MIC (#FE bit 3) through `kg_tape_monitor` of modules (a 1 held over 37 ms relaxes to 0).
- `src/rtl/vg93`: the FDC of the Go, taken from karabas-go-core-profi (keep the two in step): the VG93 model (github.com/demyanenko-d/FD1793_1818VG93, `vg93_config.vh` its build options), `fdd_glue.v` (data separator, write precompensation), `hrdy_fdc.v` (port #FF, WAIT of the CPU: `busy` to WAIT_n of the T80 after a register write and to pace the ports in turbo; here with the INTRQ / DRQ outputs for zports). The decoders are in `zports.v` (`vg_cs_n`, `vg_csff_n`, the virtual drives of `fddvirt`). `src/rtl/uart/zifi.vhd`: ZiFi and the RS232 ports (kg_fifo).
